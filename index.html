Interactive demo

Take it for a spin.

The demo runs right here. The student account is made up, and the campus resources come from official uark.edu pages.

  1. Sign in with anythingTap Use demo account, or type any username and password.
  2. Search like you talkTry free food, I feel anxious, or pay my bill.
  3. Open a resourceSee hours, whether it's open now, location, and a link to the official page. Tap Save to keep it.
  4. Tap Help nowThe red button shows 24/7 crisis and safety lines.

Demo only. Don't enter your real UARK password. The demo doesn't check, save, or send what you type.
